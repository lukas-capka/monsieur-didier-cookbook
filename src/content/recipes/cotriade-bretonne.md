Cotriade is a humble fisherman's stew from the Breton coast, built around what came in with the boats.

## Ingredients

- 700 g mixed white fish fillets (hake, cod, pollock), cut in chunks
- 500 g waxy potatoes, sliced
- 2 leeks, cleaned and sliced
- 1 yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 bay leaf
- 4 sprigs thyme
- 750 ml fish stock
- 150 ml dry cider
- 2 tbsp butter
- Sea salt and black pepper
- Chopped parsley, to serve

## Method

1. Melt butter in a heavy pot and gently sweat onion, leek, and garlic for 8 to 10 minutes.
2. Add potatoes, bay leaf, thyme, stock, and cider. Simmer until potatoes are almost tender.
3. Add fish chunks, season lightly, and poach for 8 to 10 minutes until just cooked.
4. Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve hot with parsley and crusty bread.
