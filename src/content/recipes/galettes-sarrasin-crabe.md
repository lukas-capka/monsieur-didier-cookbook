Prepare thin buckwheat galettes in a lightly buttered pan. Keep them warm while you mix picked crab meat with creme fraiche, chives, lemon zest, and a pinch of sea salt.

Fill each galette with the crab mixture and fold into quarters. Return briefly to the pan to heat through.

Serve with a crisp green salad and Breton cider.
