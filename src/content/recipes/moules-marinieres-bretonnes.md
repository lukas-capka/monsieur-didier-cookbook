Along the Breton shore, mussels are often steamed with cider instead of white wine for a local twist.

## Ingredients

- 2 kg fresh mussels, cleaned and debearded
- 2 shallots, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 200 ml dry Breton cider
- 30 g butter
- 1 tbsp creme fraiche (optional)
- 2 tbsp chopped flat-leaf parsley
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Melt butter in a large pot and soften shallots and garlic over medium heat.
2. Add mussels and cider, cover, and steam for 5 to 7 minutes, shaking once or twice.
3. Discard any mussels that remain closed.
4. Stir in creme fraiche if using, finish with parsley and pepper, and serve immediately.
