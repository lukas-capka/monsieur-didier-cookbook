In a wide pot, sweat sliced leeks, onions, and garlic in butter. Add potato rounds, fish stock, bouquet garni, and a splash of dry white wine.

Simmer until the potatoes are tender, then nestle firm white fish pieces into the broth and poach gently.

Finish with sea salt, black pepper, and chopped parsley. Serve in warm bowls with rouille or crusty bread.
