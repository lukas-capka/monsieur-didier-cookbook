These gratinated oysters are popular in coastal brasseries during holiday season. Butter enriched with seaweed brings an iodine-rich note that echoes the Atlantic air.

## Ingredients

- 24 fresh oysters, opened and loosened from shell
- 60 g salted butter, softened
- 1 tsp dried seaweed flakes (dulse or nori), finely crushed
- 1 small garlic clove, very finely grated
- 2 tbsp finely chopped parsley
- 40 g fresh breadcrumbs
- 1 tbsp lemon zest
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Coarse sea salt, for stabilizing shells
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 230 C and set a rack in the upper third.
2. Mix butter, seaweed flakes, garlic, parsley, lemon zest, and a pinch of pepper in a small bowl.
3. Stir breadcrumbs into the butter mixture until evenly coated.
4. Spread a thick bed of coarse salt on a baking tray to hold oysters level.
5. Arrange opened oysters in their deep shells on the salt bed, keeping their liquor inside.
6. Top each oyster with a small spoonful of seaweed butter crumbs and a few drops of lemon juice.
7. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes until bubbling and lightly golden. Serve hot immediately.

## Notes

- Wear a cut-resistant glove when opening oysters, or ask your fishmonger to open them.
- Keep oyster liquor in the shell for moisture and flavor.
- If broiling at the end, watch closely to avoid overcooking.

## Pairing Suggestion

- Serve with very cold Muscadet or a Breton sparkling cider as an aperitif starter.
