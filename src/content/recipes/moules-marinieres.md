Rinse and debeard the mussels, discarding any broken shells. In a heavy pot, soften minced shallots in butter, then add dry Breton cider and bring to a simmer.

Add the mussels, cover, and steam until they open. Toss with chopped parsley and a squeeze of lemon.

Serve immediately with toasted country bread and a small bowl for shells.
