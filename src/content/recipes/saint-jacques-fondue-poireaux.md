This bistro-style plate is a Breton classic in winter, when scallops are at their sweetest. Rich leek fondue balances the delicate shellfish without hiding its flavor.

## Ingredients

- 16 large scallops, coral removed
- 3 leeks, finely sliced
- 1 shallot, minced
- 30 g salted butter
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 120 ml dry cider
- 80 ml creme fraiche
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp chopped chives
- Sea salt and freshly ground white pepper

## Steps

1. Rinse sliced leeks thoroughly in cold water to remove grit, then drain well.
2. Melt half the butter in a saute pan over medium-low heat. Add shallot and leeks with a pinch of salt, then cook gently for 10 to 12 minutes until very soft.
3. Add half the cider and simmer for 2 minutes. Stir in creme fraiche and cook 2 more minutes to form a creamy fondue. Keep warm on low heat.
4. Pat scallops completely dry and season with salt and white pepper.
5. Heat olive oil in a second pan over high heat. Sear scallops 60 to 90 seconds per side until golden outside and just translucent in the center.
6. Add remaining butter and cider to the scallop pan, swirl briefly, then finish with lemon zest and juice.
7. Spoon leek fondue onto warm plates, top with scallops, and drizzle with pan juices. Finish with chives.

## Notes

- Moist scallops will steam instead of sear, so dry them very well.
- If scallops are small, reduce searing time by 20 to 30 seconds per side.
- The leek base can be made 1 day ahead and reheated gently.

## Pairing Suggestion

- Pair with a mineral Muscadet Sevre-et-Maine or a dry Breton cider.
