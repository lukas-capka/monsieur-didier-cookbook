Mackerel is a staple fish in Brittany, and cider gives this simple sauce depth and brightness.

## Ingredients

- 4 whole mackerel, cleaned
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 shallots, minced
- 180 ml dry cider
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 30 g cold butter, cubed
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- Sea salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Score the mackerel skin lightly, season with salt and pepper, and brush with olive oil.
2. Grill over medium-high heat for 4 to 5 minutes per side until the flesh flakes.
3. In a small pan, cook shallots for 2 minutes, add cider, and reduce by half.
4. Whisk in mustard, then cold butter off heat to make a glossy sauce.
5. Spoon sauce over fish and serve with lemon wedges.
