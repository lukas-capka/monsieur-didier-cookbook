Mackerel is a staple fish in Brittany thanks to its rich flavor and affordability. A quick cider reduction with shallots cuts through the oils and gives the dish a bright, coastal character.

## Ingredients

- 4 whole mackerel, cleaned
- 2 tbsp olive oil, plus extra for grill
- 2 shallots, minced
- 200 ml dry cider
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 30 g cold butter, cubed
- 1 tbsp chopped chives
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- Sea salt and black pepper
- 1 small fennel bulb, thinly sliced (optional side)

## Steps

1. Pat the mackerel dry and score each side with three shallow diagonal cuts. Season generously with salt and pepper, then brush with olive oil.
2. Preheat a grill or grill pan to medium-high and oil the grates lightly.
3. Grill mackerel for 4 to 5 minutes on the first side, then 3 to 4 minutes on the second side, until skin is crisp and flesh flakes from the backbone.
4. While fish cooks, place shallots and cider in a small saucepan and simmer until reduced by roughly half, about 6 minutes.
5. Whisk in Dijon mustard, remove from heat, and swirl in cold butter cubes until the sauce is glossy. Stir in chives.
6. Transfer fish to a platter, spoon over sauce, and serve with lemon wedges and optional shaved fennel salad.

## Notes

- Ask your fishmonger to butterfly the mackerel if you prefer easier serving.
- Do not overcook; mackerel dries quickly once fully opaque.
- The same sauce works well with sardines or sea bream.

## Pairing Suggestion

- Pour a glass of chilled Breton cider or a crisp Albarino-style white.
